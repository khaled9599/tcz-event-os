# Authority and Approval Model

- **L0 Autonomous:** low-risk, reversible internal operations.
- **L1 Act and Report:** agent may act but must log the action.
- **L2 Human Approval:** authorized TCZ/client decision required before protected mutation.
- **L3 Qualified Professional:** structural, rigging, electrical, life-safety or other professional signoff where required.

Producing agents cannot be sole approvers of their own critical work. L3 is never downgraded because an AI is confident. Rejected changes remain in history.
