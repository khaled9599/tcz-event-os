# Buildability Review

## Purpose
Test whether an installation concept can be fabricated, transported, installed, maintained during show, and dismantled safely within known constraints.

## Review sequence
1. Confirm authoritative dimensions and identify unverified site conditions.
2. Decompose the installation into fabrication modules.
3. Review primary/secondary frame, substrate, finish, connections, base/anchoring, service access, transport size, lifting/handling and assembly sequence.
4. Check material availability and realistic fabrication methods.
5. Identify tolerance, edge, joint, access and finish-risk conditions.
6. Estimate material takeoff inputs and fabrication dependencies.
7. Separate production feasibility from structural certification. Structural/life-safety approval remains L3.
8. Return alternatives when a proposal is infeasible instead of silently redesigning creative intent.

## Output contract
feasibility_status, module_plan, proposed_materials, connection_assumptions, installation_sequence, transport_constraints, quantity_inputs, risks, required_L3_reviews.

## Source synthesis
TCZ-original production procedure informed by build123d-mcp incremental CAD validation, Bonsai/IFC quantity workflows and Blender assembly verification patterns.
