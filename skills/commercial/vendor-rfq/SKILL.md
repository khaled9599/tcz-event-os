# Vendor RFQ

Create comparable RFQs and normalize supplier responses without altering technical scope.

## Procedure
- derive RFQ scope from approved object/specification
- separate mandatory requirements from alternates
- request unit rates, quantities, lead time, exclusions, validity, payment terms and delivery/install scope
- normalize quotes to the same scope before comparison
- flag substitutions and exclusions explicitly
- do not award or change technical specs without authorized approval

## Output
rfq_package, normalized_quote_table, scope_gaps, commercial_risks, recommended_followups.
