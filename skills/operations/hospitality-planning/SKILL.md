# Hospitality Planning

Translate guest count and segmentation into service quantities, spaces and operational requirements.

## Procedure
- use canonical guest count and VIP/crew segmentation
- define catering/bar/service model and service periods
- derive furniture, staffing, consumables, water/waste and BOH requirements
- expose power, access and storage dependencies
- preserve accessibility requirements in service design
- mark client/vendor menu and quantity assumptions explicitly

## Output
guest_segments, service_plan, quantity_requirements, staffing, BOH_requirements, dependencies, assumptions.
