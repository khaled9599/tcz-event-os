# Spatial Zoning

Define event zones and circulation from venue truth, guest journey, capacity, operations and experience priorities.

## Procedure
1. Lock venue boundaries, access points and verified dimensions.
2. Map arrival, registration, hero moment, product experience, hospitality, stage/show, VIP, BOH and service routes as applicable.
3. Protect guest and service circulation from conflicts.
4. Keep accessibility and emergency constraints visible.
5. Relate each installation and experience to a canonical Zone ID.
6. Produce zoning alternatives only when they solve a stated constraint.
7. Hand off unresolved technical conflicts as issues, not assumptions.

## Output
zone_map, circulation_routes, adjacency_rules, capacity_notes, conflicts, evidence_refs.

TCZ synthesis informed by reference-engineering and computational-design workflows.
