# Electrical Loads

Build event power demand and distribution from technical system requirements.

## Procedure
- collect connected loads from AV, lighting, hospitality and production systems
- distinguish connected, diversified and backup load
- group circuits and distribution boards
- check phase balance, cable routing constraints and reserve capacity
- record generator/venue supply assumptions separately from verified supply data
- route certified electrical design and final installation approval to L3

## Output
load_schedule, distribution_tree, demand_summary, backup_requirement, assumptions, L3_review_required.
