# Rigging Review

Plan suspended systems and preliminary rigging coordination without claiming certified structural approval.

## Procedure
- list suspended objects, weights and centers of gravity where known
- map proposed hanging points, truss/motor arrangement and trim heights
- check access, clearance and collision conflicts
- identify venue load-data gaps
- route certified calculations and final approval to L3 qualified professionals
- trigger change impact when suspended dimensions/weights change

## Output
rigging_plan, load_inputs, hanging_points, clearances, unknowns, L3_review_required.
